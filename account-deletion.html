In-app deletion steps

To delete your Yustam account yourself, open the app, go to Settings > Profile > Delete Account, and follow the instructions. For assistance, contact support at support@yustam.com.ng.

Step 1
Open the YUSTAM app and sign in.
Step 2
Go to Profile or Settings and tap Delete Account.
Step 3
Confirm the request to permanently delete your account.

What happens after you request deletion

  • We confirm account ownership via email or phone.
  • Your account is deactivated immediately after confirmation.
  • Personal data is deleted from active systems within 30 days.
  • Backups are overwritten within 90 days unless legally required.

Data that will be deleted

  • Profile details such as name, photo, and contact info.
  • Saved items, listings, storefront data, and chat history.
  • Support tickets and in-app preferences.

Data we may retain

  • Transaction records required for tax, audit, or legal compliance.
  • Dispute and fraud prevention records tied to completed orders.
  • Minimal identifiers to honor opt-out and deletion requests.
